# v1.0.0 — Log Off

**Doomscrolling Against Mandatory Notifications. Log off. Violently.**

The first release of DAMN: an original, fully offline, 90s-style first-person shooter for the browser, absolutely saturated with extremely online comedy. Every wall, monster, gun, sound, song and bad joke is generated by the game's own code. There are no image files, no audio files, no engine, no frameworks and no network calls. It's powered by [VANILLAJS](https://github.com/QSOLKCB/VANILLAJS) and [OPT](https://github.com/QSOLKCB/OPT).

[Play DAMN](https://qsolkcb.github.io/DAMN/)

> The Algorithm went sentient. The comment section leaked into reality. You are the last person who reads the terms and conditions. Find the admin passwords, fight through three tabs, and hit **LOG OFF**.

## Three tabs

1. **The Comment Section.** *Where nuance goes to die.* The RED admin password is, naturally, `hunter2`.
2. **The Server Farm.** *It is always 3 AM in here.* `password123` opens the east wing.
3. **The Algorithm.** *It knows what you did. It recommended it.* Get the YELLOW password (`admin / admin`), then uninstall the boss.

Every tab has secrets:
- **Hidden walls** slide away when you press E. You'll hear dial-up.
- **Secret tiles** count toward your grade.

The intermission grades each tab from *D / SKILL ISSUE* to *S / TOUCHED ALL THE GRASS*, and asks whether you saved any screen time.

## The opposition

- **Well Actually:** a speech bubble with legs that charges in to correct you.
- **Engagement Bot:** "HELLO SIR. NICE POST! CHECK MY BIO."
- **Troll:** throws flaming hot takes. Do not feed.
- **Influencer:** a floating ring light firing volleys of hearts. "SMASH THAT LIKE BUTTON."
- **Doomscroller:** a giant phone that hasn't slept, firing rocket emojis.
- **The Algorithm:** an all-seeing eye on a pyramid of screens. It fires targeted ads and recommends more bots.

Enemies shout speech-bubble barks when they spot you. Gunfire wakes anyone who can hear it through open space. They chase you around corners and open doors to reach you.

## The arsenal

1. **Ban Hammer:** moderation in its purest form.
2. **Block Button:** silence them, reliably.
3. **Hot Take:** spicy, divisive, seven pellets.
4. **Copypasta:** the same joke, 600 times a minute.
5. **Rocket Emoji:** to the moon, with splash damage.
6. **L + Ratio:** ends discourse, permanently.

## Pickups

- **Health:** Touch Grass (+10), Hydration Check (+25), Main Character Energy (+100, up to 200).
- **Armor:** Ad-Blocker shards and the Blue Check ("you're verified now, somehow").
- **Airplane Mode:** 20 seconds of invulnerability, followed by "847 notifications incoming."
- **Keys:** admin passwords on sticky notes, for the matching coloured doors.
- **Hazards:** spicy batteries explode, and they chain.

## Four difficulties

- **Casual Scroller:** half damage, double ammo.
- **Terminally Online:** the intended experience.
- **Hasn't Touched Grass:** harder hits, more aggression.
- **Main Character Syndrome:** everyone is out to get you. They are.

## Under the hood

- A software raycaster renders chunky low-resolution pixels:
  - Textured walls and inset sliding doors with jambs.
  - Perspective floors and ceilings, and distance fog.
  - Depth-tested sprites and a weapon viewmodel.
  - A pixel-font HUD with a *VIBES* status, and an automap (Tab).
- Synthesized sound effects and an original 152 BPM chip-metal score that intensifies in fights.
- **Offline:** the service worker caches everything. After one visit, unplug the router and keep playing.
- Progress, best grades and settings are saved on your device.

## Verification

24 automated tests pass:
- A key-aware level solver proves every item is reachable and every exit needs its passwords or the boss.
- Doors, passwords and hidden walls; hitscan and occlusion; splash and chain explosions; enemy wake-up and armor; difficulty scaling; pickups; the boss; AI routing; death and restart.
- Logging off through the opened boss door.
- Deterministic replays and a CPU budget, art coverage, and a check that the pixel font can print every joke.

The CI browser check plays the packaged build in Chromium: menus, doors, firing, the automap, weapons, pause gating, all three tabs through to the victory screen, death, and an offline reload. Balance and mouse feel have not yet been playtested by a person.
